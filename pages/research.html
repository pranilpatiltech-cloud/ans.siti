Research & Innovation

Advancing sustainable infrastructure through cutting-edge research

Materials and Digital Construction

ANS–SITI brings together structural engineering, construction materials and digital fabrication. The team’s research interests include sustainable printable materials and circular infrastructure solutions.

Printable Construction Materials

Development of materials for 3D concrete printing, including recycled construction resources and alternative binder systems.

Low-Carbon Infrastructure

Materials and prefabricated systems that support lower-carbon, resource-efficient construction.

Research into Practice

Connecting material development and digital manufacturing with physical construction elements, including the concrete benches shown in our product gallery.
