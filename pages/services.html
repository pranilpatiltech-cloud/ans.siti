Construction Technology Focus Areas

ANS–SITI works on construction materials and technologies at IIT Dharwad, Karnataka. Our focus connects material development, resource reuse and digital fabrication for sustainable infrastructure.

Sustainable Construction Materials

Material development focused on durability and lower environmental impact, including alternatives to conventional construction materials.

3D Concrete Printing

Additive manufacturing approaches that connect printable construction materials with digitally designed concrete elements.

Circular Economy

Construction waste recycling and material reuse to support resource-efficient infrastructure.

Digital Fabrication

Digital construction methods and prefabricated systems that connect design, materials and manufacturing.
